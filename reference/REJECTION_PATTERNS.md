# The five rejection patterns (what 140+ pre-registered tests on this universe taught us; cite the number when a proposal risks one)

**1. Ranks without dollars.** Strong rank-IC (t 3–10), monotone quintiles — and a negative net spread. Two causes: (a) log-return spreads are the variance drag of high-vol names (a +283 bp/week log spread was +6 bp in simple returns); (b) the shorts pay negative funding — high-vol, small, "trashy" names run negative funding, i.e. they are FundingCarry's long cohort. Defence: the battery's target is the simple-return BTC-residual net of funding (K5); any feature that is MAX / IVOL / volume volatility / "quality" / Corwin–Schultz spread in disguise ranks well and earns nothing.

**2. Calibration-only.** Passes 2022–23 at t ≥ 2.5, flat or wrong-signed 2024–26. Common for anything built on retail positioning or early-listing dynamics. Defence: K1 requires the eval window same-sign at t ≥ 1.5; RESEARCH FURTHER requires eval net dollars ≥ 0.

**3. Sleeve in disguise.** Residual IC after a4 (funding sort), a12 (size / volume / illiquidity), a90 (7-day taker flow), log MDV and log mcap falls below 50% of raw. Price-based liquidity, scheduled supply inflation, "trend + momentum + carry stacks", premium-level sorts and most "quality" factors are a12 or a4 relabelled. Defence: K3; name the nearest sleeve and the expected correlation in the proposal.

**4. One tercile, one year, one event.** The spread lives in names below $20M MDV (uncapturable at size), in a single year, or in a handful of events (listing squeezes, one pump/dump episode, the 2025-Q3 → 2026-Q2 premium episode). Defence: the battery reports MDV-tercile, year and quintile-mean tables; robustness r3/r7/r8/r9 for CANDIDATE track.

**5. Placebo-equivalent.** A fixed random relabelling of names, or the same signal led by L days, predicts as well as the real feature; or the placebo cannot be computed (NaN) — which is a FAIL, never a vacuous pass. Typical of features that respond to returns rather than predict them (wick absorption, chasing flow, display-rollover effects). Defence: K4; lead/lag in whole weeks for persistent characteristics.

Secondary patterns worth naming in a critique: **trend is reversal at the weekly horizon in this universe** (six tests, every residual form); **day-boundary / settlement-hour flow is < 2 bp** (an FTX-era artefact); **cross-venue lead/lag exists only at the hourly scale and is ≤ 1.3 bp**; **the book is a quiet-market strategy** (Sharpe 2.7–3.3 in low-dispersion terciles vs 0.9–1.4 high) but a dispersion multiplier adds nothing the CPPI governor does not already do; **the large-cap basket trails BTC every year** — that bleed is the short leg's alpha, not a regime to time (six tests, do not reopen without a forced-flow mechanism and new data).
